COMFORT WITHOUT OSTENTATION

The lodge sits at Lago do Maçarico and follows a language inspired by riverside communities: timber, boardwalks, private chalets, regional meals and close contact with the forest.

The journey from Manaus is part of the experience, connecting water, road, forest and local ways of life without invented distances or artificial promises.

Private chalets and regional meals.

The idea is to offer a comfortable base for days of trails, canoes, sunrise outings, forest nights and simple rest after activities.
